    perf(qmdb): cache the upper tree and refresh only the paths of changed twig roots
    
    root() rebuilt the whole upper tree on every call and prove() rebuilt it again
    for each proof. The levels are now kept behind a mutex (root() stays &self):
    a root read compares the leaf level with the twig roots and rehashes only the
    ancestors of the ones that changed, and a change of power-of-two capacity
    rebuilds. prove() reads its siblings from the cached levels. The full fold is
    kept as root_full() and asserted equal in debug builds, and a test crosses
    capacities 1-8 forward and back through undo.
    
    Roots unchanged: gov5 cross-client vectors, the real node state, harness S and
    L workloads. Block latency at 50M keys is unchanged (the fold was a few ms);
    proofs no longer pay a rebuild each.
